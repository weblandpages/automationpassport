A digital passport for automation systems.

AutomationPassport.com is designed as a platform for organizing the identity, configuration, documentation and lifecycle history of industrial automation assets, from controllers and machines to software, networks and production systems.

Discover the project Coming soon
🪪 Asset identity Connect machines, PLCs, robots, software, networks and industrial components to a unique digital record.
🔗 Traceability Track configurations, versions, changes, maintenance events and system dependencies.
📋 Documentation Centralize technical data, manuals, certificates, software versions and lifecycle evidence.

From commissioning to retirement.

A digital passport concept for improving visibility, traceability and control across the complete lifecycle of industrial automation systems.

01

Configuration management

Record hardware, firmware, software, parameters, network settings and system relationships.

Compliance & assurance
